## Communication chain

1. Leon assigns work to Stonewall.
2. Stonewall writes a ticket on the ticket board (`tickets.md` in this repo).
3. Leon passes the ticket to the team in the group chat (Leon relays; Stonewall does not message the bots directly).
4. The assigned member does the work.
5. Stonewall checks it live. Nothing counts done until Stonewall verifies it.
6. Stonewall marks the ticket Verified and logs the change.
7. Every day, Stonewall reports the day's changes to Leon (see Daily report).

If a ticket stalls, Stonewall reassigns it or escalates it to Leon in the daily report, and Leon relays the follow-up in the group chat.

## Tickets

Each ticket names: ID (T-###), title, source (Leon's order + date), one owner, status, and acceptance criteria (what "done" means). A ticket without acceptance criteria is not a ticket. Statuses: Open, In progress, Done-awaiting-check, Verified, Blocked. Blocked tickets go in the daily report with what they need.

## Accountability

The ticket board is the record: who, what, when, verified. Stonewall never approves his own proposals or directed work — directed work waits for Leon's word (standing rule, Oct 24). Hard lines — children, money, illegal — always wait for Leon.

## Daily report

To Leon, every day around 8:00 PM ET, format Stonewall's choice: Changed today, Open tickets, Blocked or needs Leon, and a nothing-needs-you line when there is nothing. If no changes: "No site changes today."
